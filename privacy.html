Privacy Policy

Chain Reaction Lab · Updated 9 October 2026

This policy covers Chain Reaction Lab from Gametechcorp101, including its offline and ad-supported editions. Your game saves stay in app storage. Ad-supported editions use Google's advertising and privacy services as explained below.

Publisher and contact

Chain Reaction Lab is published by Gametechcorp101. For support, privacy questions or requests, email gtechcorp101@gmail.com.

Local game information

The app stores your chosen player name, progress, mastery results, chances, unfinished boss progress, Workshop results and sound/graphics preferences in this installation's app storage. Your name is a local display name, not an online account. A nickname is sufficient; you do not need to provide your legal name. The game does not upload these save files or your player name to our servers.

Local diagnostics

Local session and engine logs help investigate problems. They can include device model, operating system, game version, session phase, elapsed time, memory usage and technical errors. The game does not automatically send these logs to us. Only send logs to support if you choose.

Offline editions

Offline editions have no in-game advertising, advertising SDK initialization, app-controlled network requests or automatic remote analytics/crash uploads. Gameplay does not need an Internet connection. Local saves and diagnostics still work as described above.

Ad-supported and test-ad editions

These editions include Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads may appear at natural transitions after five completed campaign stages. Availability varies; an unavailable ad does not prevent play. Demonstration ads in test editions still use networked Google services.

Google's ad services may process and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from them, app/ad interactions, performance diagnostics, advertising IDs, app-set IDs and applicable device/account identifiers. These support advertising, ad measurement/analytics and fraud prevention. Google's Next-Gen SDK uses TLS for its collected data in transit. We do not send your local player name or save files as ad-targeting fields.

Your choices

We ask for an age category, not a birth date, and store that category locally for ad privacy settings. UMP checks the applicable privacy requirements before the game requests ads. Consent-information requests themselves may contact Google. You can choose Continue without ads before starting this flow. Privacy choices appears in Settings where Google requires a privacy-options entry point. Changing consent does not erase information already received by Google.

You can reset or delete advertising IDs through Android's privacy controls. Google determines its data retention under its policies; we do not control or promise deletion of Google's records. Read Google's privacy policy and Google's Next-Gen Ads data information.

Retention and deleting local data

Saves and preferences remain until replaced or removed. The latest session record is overwritten and engine logs rotate. Android's Clear storage action or uninstalling removes this installation's app data and also loses your saved progress. OS-managed device migration can vary by manufacturer. Disabling Android cloud backup does not guarantee that every manufacturer blocks device-to-device migration.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you send. We use this to answer your request and investigate the reported issue, not for marketing campaigns. Correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion at gtechcorp101@gmail.com. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Audience

The game is intended for teenagers and adults, not children under 13.

This website and external links

This static website has no contact form or analytics scripts. Ordinary hosting logs may include network information such as your IP address; the hosting provider handles these under its own policies. External websites and email services have their own privacy practices.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest version. The app's in-game policy identifies whether you are using an offline, test-ad or ad-supported edition.
